# Common Tone — Privacy policy

*Last updated: 13 November 2026*

Common Tone is a chord app for piano and guitar by Agus Kresna.

**Common Tone collects no personal data and shares nothing.** It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting service, and it doesn't connect to the internet.

Everything you do in the app stays on your phone: your settings, favorite and recent chords, saved progressions, lesson progress and ear-training results. They are stored in the app's own storage and removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

On the first launch on Android, the app reads the country of your SIM card or mobile network (no permission needed) only to choose the starting language. It isn't stored or sent anywhere.

The app is for a general audience. It has no sign-up and no way to contact other people.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Questions: see the contact address in the app's store listing.
